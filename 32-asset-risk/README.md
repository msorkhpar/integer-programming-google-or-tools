# 7.3. Asset Risk

Where the risk numbers in a capital plan come from: an asset's Weibull failure curve and its hazard,
what a remaining-useful-life prediction is and is not, the consequence of a failure and risk as an
expected cost, and the risk an intervention removes in each year, summed into the coefficient a
start-year model can use.

GridCap, the portfolio every example uses, is a fictional utility generated for this course. Its
assets, curves and costs describe no real company.
