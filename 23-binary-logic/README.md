# 6.1. Binary Logic

Yes/no decisions as the language of planning rules: counting funded projects with sums of binaries,
writing NOT, AND, OR, XOR and implication as linear rows, at-least, at-most and exactly-k rules and
the switch that allows "none, or at least k", and replacing products of binaries, or of a binary
and a bounded continuous variable, by exact linear rows.
