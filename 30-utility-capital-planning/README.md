# 7.1. Utility Capital Planning

The domain the course's models serve: how a regulated utility plans and recovers capital (programs,
portfolios, rate cases, rate base and revenue requirement), what its transmission, distribution and
vegetation fleets are and which model rules each brings, how a plan moves through the planning
cycle from Initial to Budget Lock as scenarios that are copied, overridden, rerun and locked, and
what planners, finance and regulators each need from the optimizer, written down as a
stakeholder-requirements worksheet. GridCap, the running example, is a fictional utility.
