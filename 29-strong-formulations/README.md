# 6.7. Strong Formulations

How to tell a strong formulation from a weak one and how to make a weak one stronger: comparing
formulations by the region their LP relaxations allow and by the bound that region gives, tightening
variable bounds and adding valid rows derived from the model's own rows, breaking the symmetry of
identical crews and jobs, and an audit of the GridCap medium model that applies all of it and
measures what each change buys from the solver.
