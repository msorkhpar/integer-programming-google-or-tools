# 8.1. Weighted Sums

Two objectives folded into one: what the weights of a weighted sum mean (a price between the
objectives, which depends on their units), why the plan jumps between a few plans as the weights
move, and why a weighted sum can never return most of the Pareto-optimal plans of an integer
program, with GridCap small's NPV and risk objectives as the running example.

GridCap, the portfolio every example uses, is a fictional utility generated for this course. Its
projects, assets and budgets describe no real company.
