# 9.2. Sensitivity for MIP

What one more dollar of budget is worth when the plan is a MILP: why a solver reports no dual
values for it and what a dual of a MILP is instead (a function, not a price), what the prices of
the fixed-integer LP mean and when they can be trusted, how to build the budget-value curve by
repeated solves and find its step points exactly, and how to tell finance what the next dollar
buys. Everything runs on GridCap's small portfolio, a fictional utility's data.
