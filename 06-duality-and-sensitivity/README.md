# 2.3. Duality and Sensitivity

Every LP comes with a second LP, its dual, whose variables price the first one's limits. This module
builds the dual by hand, proves a plan optimal with it, reads shadow prices, reduced costs and their
ranges from a solver, and turns those numbers into answers a finance partner can use. The running
model is the GridCap budget LP: three programs, three years, crews, carry-over and a vegetation
floor, on the fictional GridCap utility's tiny portfolio.
