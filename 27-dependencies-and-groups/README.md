# 6.5. Dependencies and Groups

The rules that tie projects to each other and to the plan as a whole: must-do projects as equality
rows and mandates on groups, with their cost; parent-child links, all-or-nothing bundles and bundles
that start together; repair and replace as mutually exclusive alternatives, with "do nothing" as a
priced option and sequences allowed by year; precedence with minimum and maximum lags; floors,
caps and shares on portfolio and jurisdictional spend; and manual overrides as bounds that can be
priced, checked and undone.
